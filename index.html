Who We Are

Committed to excellence in legal services

Azura, Faizah & Associates (AFA), Advocates & Solicitors / Syarie Counsel, was established on 1st January 2023. The firm was founded by Azura Binti Abdul Aziz and Nur Faizah Binti Zainidin, proud alumnae of Ahmad Ibrahim Kulliyyah of Laws, International Islamic University Malaysia (IIUM), who were called to the Bar in 1999 and 2002 respectively.

Throughout the years, both partners have worked closely with clients ranging across public and private sectors, corporate bodies, banks, financial institutions, housing developers, and real estate agencies — across a wide range of real estate matters and financing documentation.

With the vast experience and exposure of both partners in handling property transactions for over 20 years, clients can be rest assured that every task and instruction is undertaken with due care and diligence. At AFA, we try our best to offer a practical, yet cost-effective solution to our clientele.

Professional Indemnity Insurance. The firm is protected under Pacific & Orient Insurance Co. up to RM300,000.00 (mandatory), and Allianz General Insurance Company up to RM4,700,000.00, valid until 31/12/2026.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers before you ask

A few common questions about our firm, our practice areas, and how to work with us.

Is Azura, Faizah & Associates a registered law firm?
Yes. The firm is registered with the Malaysian Bar under Section 85(1) of the Legal Profession Act 1976, Registration Number 000020007489, certified 9 January 2023. Details appear in the Certificate of Registration section above.
What areas of law does the firm practice?
Our core practice covers Property & Real Estate / Conveyancing, Islamic Banking & Financing, Contract & Agreement, Administration & Probate / Faraid & Hibah / Will, Asset Solution & Estate Planning, Muslim Family Law / Shariah Matters, and Legal Advisory & Consultancy.
Do you handle LPPSA housing loan matters?
Yes. LPPSA (Lembaga Pembiayaan Perumahan Sektor Awam) is a main highlight of our practice — we sit on its panel and act regularly on public sector housing loan documentation.
Which banks and institutions do you act for?
Our clients include Public Islamic Bank Berhad / Public Bank Berhad, PUNB, Bank Islam Malaysia Berhad, RHB Islamic Bank, private developers, IM Global Property Consultants Sdn Bhd, Neo Prop Realty, Almaventures Development Sdn Bhd, and Jabatan Perumahan Negara under the Kementerian Perumahan & Kerajaan Tempatan.
Can you assist with Faraid, Hibah or estate disputes?
Yes. Beyond documentation and advisory work on estate management and planning, we also act as Mediator in estate distribution and Faraid disputes.
Is the firm insured?
Yes. We are protected by Professional Indemnity Insurance under Pacific & Orient Insurance Co. up to RM300,000.00 (mandatory), and Allianz General Insurance Company up to RM4,700,000.00, valid until 31/12/2026.
How are your legal fees determined?
Our fees are tailored according to your needs and kept reasonable within the Solicitors Remuneration Orders 2023. Reach out to us directly for a quote specific to your matter.
What are your office hours, and where are you located?
We are open Monday to Friday, 8:30 am to 5:30 pm, and Saturday by appointment (closed Sundays), at No. 9-1, 1st Floor, Jalan Uranus AG U5/AG, Section U5, 40150 Shah Alam, Selangor Darul Ehsan.
